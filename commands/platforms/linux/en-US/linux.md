## Purpose

Entry point for common Linux commands covering files, text, permissions, processes, storage, networking, services, logs, and Debian-family package management.

## Common commands

- Files and directories: pwd, mkdir, rmdir, touch, cp, mv, rm, ln
- Text: cat, less, head, tail, grep, find
- Users and permissions: whoami, id, chmod, chown, sudo, adduser, userdel
- Processes and system: ps, top, kill, uname, date
- Storage: df, du, free, lsblk
- Networking: ip, ping, ss
- Services and logs: systemctl, journalctl, crontab
- Packages: apt, apt-cache, dpkg
- Archives and help: tar, gzip, man, clear, echo

## Notes

Confirm targets before deleting files, changing permissions, or modifying packages. Use `man command` for full documentation.
