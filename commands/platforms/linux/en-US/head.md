## Purpose

Display the beginning of a file. Available on Debian, Ubuntu, and Linux Mint.

## Syntax

`head [选项] [文件...]`

## Common options

- -n：指定行数
- -c：指定字节数

## Examples

```bash
head -n 20 access.log
```

## More options

See `man head` or `head --help` (if supported) for additional options.
