## Purpose

Move or rename files and directories. Available on Debian, Ubuntu, and Linux Mint.

## Syntax

`mv [选项] 源 目标`

## Common options

- -i：覆盖前询问
- -n：不覆盖已有文件
- -v：显示过程

## Examples

```bash
mv old.txt archive.txt
```

## More options

See `man mv` or `mv --help` (if supported) for additional options.
