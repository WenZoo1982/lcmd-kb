## Purpose

Display process information. Available on Debian, Ubuntu, and Linux Mint.

## Syntax

`ps [选项]`

## Common options

- -e：显示全部进程
- -f：完整格式
- -u：按用户筛选
- 常用组合 aux

## Examples

```bash
ps aux
```

## More options

See `man ps` or `ps --help` (if supported) for additional options.
